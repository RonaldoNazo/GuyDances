GuyDances
=========

Double-click guydances.exe to run it.

He starts hidden. Play music (YouTube, Spotify, VLC, ...) and he appears.
When the music stops or is paused, he disappears after about 2 seconds.

- Left-drag him to move.
- Right-click him for Pause / Resume, Stop (1, 2, 4, 8 hours), Quit.

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the exe is unsigned:
click "More info" then "Run anyway".
